# House photos

Every photo in this folder shows up on the hanging photo line at the top of the website,
in file-name order.

**Upload:** on github.com open this `photos` folder, then **Add file → Upload files**,
drag your photos in, and press **Commit changes**. The site updates in about a minute.

**Captions come from the file name.** Rename a photo before uploading, for example:

- `01 Smyrna townhome - 3 bed, walk to Market Village.jpg`
- `02 Marietta ranch - big fenced backyard.jpg`

The number sets the order (it isn't shown). Text before ` - ` is the title, text after it is
the caption. Phone names like `IMG_1234.jpg` just show "Metro Atlanta home".

Use .jpg, .png or .webp, ideally under 2 MB each.
