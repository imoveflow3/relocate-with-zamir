Questions

Good to know.

Does it cost anything to work with you as a buyer?

Our first consultation is always free. How the buyer's agent gets paid is spelled out in a written buyer brokerage agreement before we tour homes, and many sellers offer to cover it. I'll walk you through all of it up front so there are no surprises.

How much do I need for a down payment?

Less than most people think. FHA loans start at 3.5% down, some conventional loans at 3%, and Georgia has down-payment help programs for qualifying buyers. A lender can tell you what you qualify for, and I can connect you with a few.

How do you decide what my home should list for?

I compare recently sold, pending and active homes like yours nearby, then adjust for condition, upgrades and the current market. You get the full report, not just a number.

Can you help with rental properties?

Yes. I can help you find a rental or lease out your property. Leasing and management services run through my sponsoring brokerage, as Georgia law requires.
